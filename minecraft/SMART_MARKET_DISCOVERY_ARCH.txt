# SMART MARKET DISCOVERY ARCHITECTURE

## Overview
A multi-stage intelligence pipeline designed to identify high-velocity opportunities across a broad universe (NSE Sector Indices + Top 50 Stocks) while maintaining low CPU overhead and minimal latency by focusing deep analysis only on "Hot" instruments.

## Data Flow Diagram
```
[Universe: 50+ Symbols] 
          ↓
[STAGE-0: MARKET RADAR] (Lightweight Scan every 3-5s)
    - Metrics: Velocity, Volume Burst, Tick Acceleration
    - Output: ENERGY_SCORE (0-100)
          ↓
[STAGE-1: FOCUS ENGINE] (Load Reduction)
    - Filter: ENERGY_SCORE ≥ 65
    - Logic: Maintain TOP 2-4 HOT instruments
    - Action: Put others to SLEEP
          ↓
[STAGE-2: DEEP STRATEGY ENGINE] (Event-Driven)
    - Logic: Full OHLC, RSI, ATR, Regime Detection
    - Pipeline: ENERGY → CONFIRMATION → RISK → PAPER EXECUTION
```

## Why This Prevents Spike Misses
Traditional fixed-loop systems might miss a spike if they aren't looking at the right symbol at the right millisecond. The Market Radar acts as a "wide-angle lens" that detects sudden kinetic changes (Velocity/Volume) across the entire universe, triggering the Deep Strategy engine immediately upon an energy event rather than waiting for a slow polling loop.

## Why CPU Load Stays Low
- **Stage-0** uses primitive arithmetic on raw price/volume deltas (no heavy indicators like RSI/EMA).
- **Stage-2** only processes 2-4 symbols instead of 50+, reducing computational complexity by ~90%.
- **Event-Driven** model ensures code only executes when meaningful market movement occurs.

## Limitations & Safeguards
- **Liquidity Guard**: Only Top 50 liquid stocks and indices are scanned to avoid spread-based traps.
- **Energy Decay**: Symbols are automatically dropped from the Focus List if their Energy Score drops below 50, preventing "sticky" focus on stale trends.
- **NSE Market Lock**: System only operates during 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST.
